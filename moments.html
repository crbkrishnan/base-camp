Three traps

Read before any test

Centimetres sneaking into newton-metres

If the question gives 40 cm and you want an answer in N m, convert first: 40 cm = 0.40 m. Every year, correct physics loses marks here.

The slant distance instead of the perpendicular one

d is the perpendicular distance from the pivot to the line of action of the force — not the distance to the spot where your hand sits.

Forgetting the beam has weight

A uniform plank, rule or bar has its own weight acting at its centre. If the pivot is not at the centre, that weight makes a moment and must go into the equation.
