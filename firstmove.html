The First Move

Ten questions. You never solve any of them — you only say what the first line of your working would be. That is the part you actually get wrong.

Why this drill exists. When you drop marks, it is almost never because you didn't know the topic. It is because you didn't know what to write first, so you wrote nothing, or you wrote the wrong opening and everything after it was doomed. Ten first moves takes two minutes. Ten full solutions takes an hour. This is the cheap version, and it trains the expensive skill.
