Letters and Email

पत्र और ईमेल

A letter to the principal and a letter to your best friend share almost nothing except the paper. One has a सेवा में block and a विषय line; the other has neither. One says आप and ends आपकी आज्ञाकारी शिष्या; the other says तुम and ends तुम्हारी सहेली. Get the layout right and the marks are nearly automatic. Get one pronoun wrong halfway down and the whole letter changes person. This page is those two skills, and then the email, which is the same letter in shorter clothes.

The deal. Last topic you banked 10 out of 15. This page is out of 30, and the honest target is 21 on the first pass and 26 on the second. Marks only count if you write your answer before opening the mark scheme — otherwise you're just reading, and reading feels like learning without being learning. Type your Hindi or write it in a notebook, but write it: nobody has ever got better at letters by nodding at one.

The register switcher

Build one request, piece by piece: the pronoun, the verb ending, the salutation and the sign-off. The colours show which level of respect each piece belongs to. They have to match. Long answers

Stuck? Take one hint, not the whole solution. Hint 1 chunks the job, hint 2 names the rule, hint 3 narrows it to the word — none of them writes the Hindi for you. Q14 and Q15 are the two that separate a 21 from a 26. Get someone who reads Hindi to look at those two.

Five traps

Read before any test

“आप … तुम …” in one letter

आदरणीय प्रधानाचार्य जी, तुम मुझे अवकाश दो। The salutation is respectful and the request is not. The instinct is to fix only the first line, because the salutation is the line you think about. The examiner reads every line. Pick the level once, at the salutation, and carry it through every pronoun, every verb ending and the sign-off.

Writing the letter as a boy

आपका आज्ञाकारी शिष्य and मैं नहीं आ सकता are both wrong when you are the writer. Two things move: the noun शिष्य → शिष्या, and the possessive आपका → आपकी, because आपका/आपकी copies the thing owned (शिष्या), not the person addressed. Every ‘I’ verb needs a feminine ending: सकूँगी, आऊँगी, चाहती हूँ. Circle each one before you finish.

Adding parts to the wrong letter

A letter to a friend that starts सेवा में and has a विषय line is a formal letter wearing a friendly hat. And a complaint to an officer with no विषय line has lost a mark for nothing. Ask one question first: would I say आप or तुम to this person? That decides the whole skeleton.

A sentence where the विषय line should be

विषय: मुझे छुट्टी चाहिए। is a request, not a subject line. A विषय line is a short phrase that names the letter, and it ends with a stock ending: विषय: दो दिन के अवकाश हेतु प्रार्थना पत्र। No ‘मैं’, no ‘मुझे’, and it never says पत्र alone.

Trusting the keyboard

Typing Hindi with Roman letters is a guess by the software. It does not know you are a girl, or that you meant की and not कि, or मैं and not में. It offers a list; the wrong ending is often first. Read what you typed once, out loud, before you send, and check every word that ends in a vowel sign.
