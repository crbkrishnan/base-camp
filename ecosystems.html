Everything Eats Something

Two ideas carry this page. First: nothing in an ecosystem is independent — change one population and you change the others, sometimes in the direction you did not expect. Second: energy flows one way and leaks badly, about 90% lost at every step, while nutrients go round in circles. Get those two straight and the rest is vocabulary.

Five traps

Read before any test

“Energy is recycled in an ecosystem”

It is not. Energy flows through — in as sunlight, up the levels, out as heat — and is never used again, which is why the Sun has to keep supplying more. Nutrients cycle: carbon, nitrogen and mineral ions go round for ever, and decomposers are the return leg. Swapping these two words is the most expensive mistake in this unit.

Drawing the arrows backwards

The arrow points from the organism eaten to the organism eating it, because that is the way the energy travels. “Fox eats rabbit” makes people draw fox → rabbit, which says the fox feeds the rabbit. Read every arrow as “gives its energy to” and you will never get it wrong again.

Using habitat, population, community and ecosystem as if they meant the same thing

Habitat is the place. A population is one species. A community is all the species. An ecosystem is the community plus the non-living surroundings, interacting. Defining an ecosystem without the non-living part is the standard way to lose a definition mark.

“Remove the predator and the prey will thrive”

Only at first. The prey population rises, eats through its own food supply, and then crashes. Predator and prey numbers cycle, with the predator peak always after the prey peak. In a graph question, point at that lag — it is what the question is built to test.

Expecting a pyramid of numbers always to be pyramid-shaped

One oak tree supports 500 caterpillars, so the bottom bar is the narrowest one. A pyramid of numbers counts organisms and ignores their size, so a single huge producer, or a few large hosts carrying many parasites, turns it upside down. A pyramid of biomass measures total mass and comes out the right way up.
